I am a PhD student at the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology working on gene regulation in the marine chordate *Oikopleura dioica*. My research combines genomics, single-cell transcriptomics, epigenomics, and reproducible software workflows to study how gene regulation is conserved despite extensive genome rearrangement.

I also build tools for the lab and community, including genome browsers, data analysis pipelines, and tutorials for cloud/HPC workflows. My background is in biological sciences from Osaka University, where I worked on NF-κB transcriptional regulation in B cells.

## Research interests

- Gene regulation and enhancer biology
- Genome evolution and genome scrambling
- Single-cell and multi-omics analysis
- Reproducible workflows with R, Python, bash, Nextflow, Docker, and Singularity

## Selected highlights

- Current PhD research at OIST on *Oikopleura dioica* genomics and regulatory evolution
- Built a browser for *O. dioica* genome data: [oikobrowser.jnicolaus.com](https://oikobrowser.jnicolaus.com)
- Published research articles, reviews, and a book chapter on transcriptional regulation and genome analysis
